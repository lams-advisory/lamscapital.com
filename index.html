Why now

Up to €10 millionor 2% of worldwide turnover: the ceiling for fines on essential entities under the NIS2 Directive. For important entities: €7 million or 1.4%.
Management is accountableBoards must approve cybersecurity measures, oversee them and take training. Responsibility cannot be delegated to IT alone.
24 hoursto send an early warning to the authority after a significant incident, 72 hours for the notification and one month for the final report.

Source: Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2), Articles 20, 23 and 34. National laws set the details; in Romania, fines have been applied since September 2026.

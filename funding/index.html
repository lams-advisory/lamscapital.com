EU funding for digitalisation

Use EU funding for digitalisation, without risking the project.

EU cohesion funds 2021–2027 co-finance the digitalisation of small and medium-sized companies through regional programmes. We help you check eligibility and prepare the technical side, and we can deliver the software if we are selected as supplier.

What we do

In Romania, through the regional programmes for SME digitalisation; in other member states, through their national and regional programmes.

Eligibility

We check your activity codes, size and region against the applicant guide, including excluded sectors.

Technical preparation

Digital maturity audit, specifications and a realistic software budget, ready for the application.

Implementation

If selected as supplier, we deliver the software and the paperwork the funding authority asks for.

Our rules

  • One role per project. In a funded project we are either the consultant or the supplier, never both, as the rules on conflicts of interest require.
  • No promises of funding. The decision belongs to the managing authority. We tell you honestly whether an application has a chance.
  • The schedule comes first. Funding calls have fixed windows. We tell you upfront whether the timing works, and what to do if it does not.

How we work

A method you can check week by week.

Every engagement follows the same structure: a fixed-scope start, written deliverables at set points, and sign-off before the next stage. Below is what you receive, when, and what it looks like.

Strategic diagnostic: five weeks

Built on your own numbers, under a non-disclosure agreement signed before any data is shared.

Week 1Kick-off with management, data request, interviews with the people who run sales, purchasing, warehouse and finance.
Week 2Performance: margin by product, client and channel; cash tied up in stock and receivables.
Week 3Market and channels: where you win, where you lose, and what competitors offer at what level of service.
Week 4Operations and opportunities: where time and money are lost, each opportunity sized and ranked.
Week 5Key indicators and a 12-month plan with owners, deadlines and budget, presented to management and signed off.

Software and automation: short stages

Nothing goes live without your acceptance. Each stage ends with a handover report you sign.

ScopeProcesses mapped as they work today, a written specification and a stage plan you approve.
BuildDelivered in stages of two to four weeks, each tested on your own data before acceptance.
Go-liveData migration, user training and a parallel-run period, so the business never stops.
SupportA monthly subscription: fixes, updates, a written monthly report and a timesheet.

Situations we are asked to solve

The problems that bring companies to us, and how we approach them.

Growth without margin

Turnover rises, cash does not. We show margin by client, product and channel, and which sales cost more than they bring.

The same order typed three times

Excel, warehouse and accounting do not talk to each other. We put orders, stock and invoicing on one system, connected to e-invoicing.

Marketplace growth, stock errors

Sales on several channels, stock updated by hand. We synchronise stock and automate invoicing across shop and marketplaces.

A security questionnaire from a key client

A large client asks for proof of cybersecurity measures. We prepare the answers and the evidence behind them.

Repetitive work that does not scale

Orders, documents and reports processed by hand. We automate the process with the best return first and measure the saving.

An investment that needs funding

A digitalisation project too large to finance alone. We check eligibility and prepare the technical side of the application.

Buying through AWS Marketplace

Buy our services through your company's AWS account.

Besides a direct contract, you can buy any LAMS package as a private offer on AWS Marketplace, the Amazon Web Services catalogue for software and services. Amazon Web Services invoices your company and pays us.

Why companies choose it

A private offer is an offer on AWS Marketplace made only for your company, on the terms we agreed.

A familiar supplier

The invoice comes from Amazon Web Services, a supplier your accounting and procurement teams can verify easily.

One place to pay

Payment goes through your company's AWS account, by card or bank transfer where available, with the invoices in one place.

Full paperwork

Every month you receive an activity report, a timesheet and a handover report that cites the AWS offer and invoice number.

How it works

1. Agree the workWe sign a non-disclosure agreement and agree the statement of work: scope, deliverables, schedule and terms.
2. Open the accountIf your company has no AWS account, we set it up together in a 30–45 minute online session, in the company's name and with two-step sign-in.
3. Accept the offerWe send the private offer. You accept it in your AWS account; the statement of work is attached to it.
4. DeliveryWe deliver in stages. Each month you receive the report, timesheet and handover document to sign.

Privacy notice

LAMS respects the confidentiality of everyone who contacts us. This notice explains how we handle personal data received through this website.

Data we collect

Only what you send us through the contact form or by email: your name, company, work email, country and the content of your message. If you agree, we also record your consent to receive occasional updates.

How we use it

To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal, as steps taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Only with your consent, to send occasional updates on NIS2, digitalisation and funding (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). We never sell your data or share it for third-party marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Service providers

We rely on carefully selected providers for hosting, form delivery and email, bound by data-processing terms. Where data is processed outside the European Economic Area, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention

Enquiry data is kept for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless an engagement follows. Records of consent are kept for as long as we need to demonstrate it.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you may object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

Client engagements

Information shared during an engagement is protected by a non-disclosure agreement and, where personal data is involved, by a data processing agreement under Article 28 GDPR.
